# BILLIE FOR YOUR MOOD

## Whatever you're feeling, there's a Billie song for that.

How are you feeling today?

BILLIE FOR YOUR MOOD es una experiencia para descubrir canciones de Billie Eilish a partir de cómo te sientes. Elige una emoción y continúa explorando el mood que mejor describe tu día.

[PLAY YOUR MOOD →](#emotions)

---

## Emotions

### HAPPY

- **Title:** HAPPY
- **Description:** For the days that feel light, fun, energetic, or simply good.

### SAD

- **Title:** SAD
- **Description:** For the moments when you need to feel the sadness instead of pushing it away.

### ANGRY

- **Title:** ANGRY
- **Description:** For frustration, irritation, release, and the moments when you've had enough.

### IN LOVE

- **Title:** IN LOVE
- **Description:** For attraction, tenderness, obsession, and the feeling of not being able to stop thinking about someone.

### HEARTBROKEN

- **Title:** HEARTBROKEN
- **Description:** For endings, rejection, loss, and the things that are still difficult to let go.

### ANXIOUS

- **Title:** ANXIOUS
- **Description:** For overthinking, restlessness, uncertainty, and the moments when your mind won't slow down.


### LONELY

- **Title:** LONELY
- **Description:** For the moments when you feel distant from everyone else or simply want someone close.


### NOSTALGIC

- **Title:** NOSTALGIC
- **Description:** For memories, old versions of yourself, people you miss, and moments you wish you could revisit.

### CONFUSED

- **Title:** CONFUSED
- **Description:** For mixed feelings, unclear relationships, and the moments when nothing seems to make complete sense.

### OVERWHELMED

- **Title:** OVERWHELMED
- **Description:** For the days when everything feels like too much and you need space from the noise.


### NUMB

- **Title:** NUMB
- **Description:** For disconnection, emptiness, and the moments when you don't really know what you're feeling.


### CONFIDENT

- **Title:** CONFIDENT
- **Description:** For feeling powerful, independent, sure of yourself, and completely done explaining who you are.

### CALM

- **Title:** CALM
- **Description:** For quiet moments, soft thoughts, breathing room, and the feeling of being in your own world.

### LONGING

- **Title:** LONGING
- **Description:** For wanting someone or something that feels distant, unfinished, or just out of reach.

---

## About

BILLIE FOR YOUR MOOD explores Billie Eilish's music through emotion instead of chronology. The experience begins with a feeling and later connects that feeling to more specific moods and songs.

This is an independent student project inspired by Billie Eilish's discography.
